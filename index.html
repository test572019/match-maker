How the score works

Each pair is scored on eight weighted dimensions (total 100). Similarity is rewarded where research shows it predicts stability (values, goals, conflict handling); balance is rewarded for traits like emotional stability and attachment security.

Core values (25)Religiosity, tradition, family closeness, money habits, ambition
Life goals (15)Children, living arrangement with family, relocation/city
Personality (15)Big Five: openness, conscientiousness, agreeableness, extraversion; neuroticism penalty when both are high
Conflict & love style (15)Conflict-handling pairing and love-language overlap
Attachment (10)Secure/anxious/avoidant/fearful pairing matrix
Lifestyle (10)Tidiness, activity, sleep rhythm, smoking
Interests (5)Shared interests overlap
Practical fit (5)Age gap, education, each person’s preferred age range

Deal-breakers flagged by either person cap the result at 35%. The raw score is then calibrated (stretched) so the percentage separates strong and weak pairs more clearly.

This is a decision-support tool built on general relationship research, not a guarantee of outcome. Use the explanations in a human interview, and handle personal data with consent.
